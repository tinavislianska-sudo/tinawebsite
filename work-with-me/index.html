Before we start

Questions you might have

Do I need to know exactly what the problem is?

No. Tell me what you’re seeing. We’ll discuss whether a review would help and what information we can work with.

Is this strategy or implementation?

I can help with both. Before we start, we agree what I’ll do and what needs your team, a developer or another specialist.

Do we have to commit to ongoing work?

No. You can book a review or a single sprint. Continuing is a separate decision.

Can you guarantee a growth target?

I can commit to the work we agree on. I can’t promise a revenue or customer number before we’ve tested what works for your product.
